Hypothesis Testing Summary - Task 4

1. Objective: 
To determine if there is a statistically significant difference in the Average Order Value (Total Sales) between Male and Female customers.

2. Statistical Test Performed: 
Independent Two-Sample T-Test (using Python and SciPy).

3. Hypotheses:
- Null Hypothesis (H0): There is no significant difference in spending based on gender.
- Alternative Hypothesis (H1): There is a significant difference in spending based on gender.

4. Results:
- T-statistic: 1.45 
- P-value: 0.15

5. Business Conclusion: 
Since the p-value (0.15) is greater than 0.05, we fail to reject the Null Hypothesis. This indicates that gender does not significantly impact the average spending amount. 

6. Recommendation: 
Marketing campaigns should prioritize targeting based on high-performing Cities and Age groups, rather than creating separate ad campaigns purely based on gender.
